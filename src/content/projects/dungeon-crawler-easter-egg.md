A small dungeon-crawler easter egg hiding behind the "▸ psst..." link on this site's [About page](/about/). Click it to reveal a tiny grid-based dungeon.

Features:
- Three connected rooms, walked between via doors.
- HP-based combat: fights play out as a back-and-forth exchange rather than an instant win/lose, and fainting just respawns you at the start of the room (no punishing restart).
- A key hidden behind an early encounter, needed to get through a locked door later on.
- A simple inventory that tracks what you're carrying.
- A boss fight as the payoff for making it through.

The whole thing resets each time you close and reopen it, so it's always ready for another run.
