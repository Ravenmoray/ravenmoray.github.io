A browser-based arcade with ten free classic games spanning reflex (Snake, Flappy), puzzle (Tetris, 2048), strategy (Tic-Tac-Toe, Minesweeper), memory, and multiplayer (Pong) genres.

Everything runs instantly in the browser — no installs, no accounts. Scores save locally, several games support local two-player or AI opponents, and difficulty is adjustable where it makes sense. Fully open source.
